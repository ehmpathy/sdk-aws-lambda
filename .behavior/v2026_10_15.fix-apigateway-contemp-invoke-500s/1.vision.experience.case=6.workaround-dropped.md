# case 6 — the workaround dropped across a suite (densest walk)

## narrative

kai owns `svc-notifications`. she runs the declapract upgrade that carries #650, and its codemod
deletes `struct: { payload: 'ancient' }` from all 18 of her api-gateway call sites. last time those
same calls 500'd without the line. she reruns both suites, the referenced and the serialized-local
one:

- her 200 cases: green — same status, same body
- her 500 cases: green — same redacted body, and now a `correlationId` that matches the exid she
  passed
- her 400 cases: red, and each failure says why — the body reads `error.class: 'ConstraintError'`
  where her assertion expected `errorType: 'BadRequestError'`. the sdk opted her in, so she got
  contemp. she rewrites those assertions to the envelope, or puts `struct: { payload: 'ancient' }`
  back on the few that must pin the browser's bytes

one pass of her suite crosses the header default, normalization, the trail read, validation, both
error builders, and the serialized hydration — every boundary this change touches.
