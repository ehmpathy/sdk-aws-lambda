# case=4 — a maintainer who drops the primitive arm goes red

critipath · sharp · actor = sdk maintainer

## narrative

a later maintainer "simplifies" `FrozenDeep` and drops the primitive arm — the same shape 0.7.0
shipped. the type check fails on the clamp in `setEventFrozen.test.ts`, which names the cause: a
frozen `IsoTimeStamp` is not assignable to `IsoTimeStamp`. the regression never reaches a consumer.

it fails safe on all three counts:
- **fast** — at `tsc`, the first gate of every local run and every CI run
- **loud** — `TS2322` on the clamp line, and the message spells the culprit: a readonly bag of
  `toString` / `charAt` / … / `_dglo` where a string was owed (measured on 0.7.0, see the yield)
- **teaches the fix** — the clamp's `.why` comment names the arm it guards and why a primitive takes
  it, so the maintainer who reads the red line reads the remedy beside it

## timeline

```
given('[case4] the clamp: freeze `{ at: IsoTimeStamp }`, assign `frozen.at` to an `IsoTimeStamp`')
  when('[t0] the primitive arm is present')
    then('tsc is green')
  when('[t1] the primitive arm is reverted')
    then('tsc fails on the clamp line')                    # the bite, observed once, recorded
  when('[t2] the arm is restored')
    then('tsc is green again')
```

after step 2 the arm lives in type-fns, where its own clamp guards it (ehmpathy/type-fns#48). the
sdk's clamp stays: it now guards that the sdk keeps a `FrozenDeep` with the arm — a re-introduced
local copy without it, or a future type-fns that drops it, goes red here too. (no released type-fns
exports `FrozenDeep` without the arm: `v1.21.4` exports none, `v1.21.5` has the arm.)
