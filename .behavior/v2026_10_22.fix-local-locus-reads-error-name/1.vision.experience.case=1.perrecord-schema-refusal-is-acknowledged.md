# case=1 — a perRecord message the schema refuses is acknowledged

**cell**: perRecord × schema × caller · sharp × criti
**seat**: the on-call engineer of the consumer team, whose pager watches the dead-letter queue

from that seat: before, a page at 3am for a message the producer broke, and no fix on our side. after, no page; at the next standup one warn in the logs names the field the producer dropped, and the engineer forwards it to the producer team.

## narrative

a producer ships a release that drops `orderId` from its `OrderPlaced` message. the `consumeOrder` endpoint, a `forSqs.perRecord`, declares `orderId` as required in `schema.input`.

before: the validator throws a `ConstraintError`, the throw fails the invoke, and sqs redrives the message. it fails the same way on each of `maxReceiveCount` receives, then lands in the dead-letter queue. the queue's alarm pages the consumer team for a fault that is the producer's.

after: the validator's `ConstraintError` is caught by class. the endpoint logs `sqs.record.refused` at warn, with the message id, the class, and the zod issue summary, and the invoke succeeds. sqs deletes the message on the first receive. the consumer team's dead-letter queue stays empty, and the warn names the field the producer dropped.
