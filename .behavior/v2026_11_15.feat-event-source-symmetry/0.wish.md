# wish: every event source has both halves, in one shape

ehmpathy/sdk-aws-lambda serves lambda event sources two ways: `genLambdaEndpoint.forX` builds a handler for a source, and `asLambdaEventSchema.fromX` gives the schema of its event. today the two sides do not match: `genLambdaEndpoint` has forApiGateway, forAsk, and forSqs (perBatch, perRecord), with no forSns and no forKinesis; `asLambdaEventSchema` has fromSns and fromKinesis, with no fromSqs.

the commander, on this: if it exists for sqs, it should exist for sns and kinesis, and vice versa. they should be super symmetric.

## outcome

- every event source the package serves has both a `genLambdaEndpoint.forX` and an `asLambdaEventSchema.fromX`, at least sqs, sns, and kinesis, in one consistent shape across sources
- `asLambdaEventSchema.fromSqs` exists, symmetric with fromSns and fromKinesis
- the symmetry law lives in the repo itself: a brief that states it, and a test that fails when a source has a forX with no fromX, or a fromX with no forX
- a source you judge should stay one-sided is not left out unsaid: it goes to the commander as an open question with your lean, in the pr body
- tests cover each new half
