# case=4 — one bad record in a kinesis batch

care: critipath · feel: sharp · inventory: c4

## .narrative

mara runs a clickstream aggregator on kinesis. a producer bug writes one malformed click into a batch
of 100. the schema validates the whole event, so one bad record refuses all 100. `forAsk` treats the
refusal as a caller fault: the invoke succeeds, and kinesis advances past the batch. 99 good clicks
are skipped with the bad one.

how it fails, and why that is the least-bad choice under `forAsk`:

- **loud** — a warn line names the error class and the bad record's path, `Records.37.kinesis.data.<field>`
  (F9). the event's sequence numbers sit in it, so mara can replay the range from the stream
- **teaches the fix** — the first lines of `fromKinesis`'s doc state the all-or-none outcome, and
  name the envelope-only form (`fromKinesis()`, decode per record in the handler) for a stream of
  mixed quality
- **the alternative is worse** — a throw would halt the shard on a poison record until it ages out
  of retention, and block every record behind it

it does not save the 99. only a per-record kinesis family can (dream `forsns-forkinesis-families`), so
F5 asks the wisher whether to ship this with the caveat or wait.
