# wish: `invoke` takes `{ headers, event, payload }` — and `rawEvent` is gone

## .the outcome

a handler at the apigateway boundary reads a request header with **no envelope hop**, and the
field that carries the full envelope is named for what it actually holds. `rawEvent` appears
nowhere in `src/`.

## .the task

> **`ehmpathy/sdk-aws-lambda#40`**
>
> ```sh
> gh issue view 40 --repo ehmpathy/sdk-aws-lambda
> ```

read it first, whole. it carries the lead shape, the two rejected alternatives with the reason
each was rejected, a **hard constraint** that any proposal must honor, the measured blast radius,
four open questions addressed to you, and the acceptance list. do not re-derive any of it.

## 🔴 .the constraint that makes this shippable standalone

the issue states it, and it is the difference between a nano and a rewrite:

> **this task changes `invoke`'s INPUT SHAPE only. it must not move what lives in `request.event`.**

`@middy/http-cors` reads `request.event.httpMethod` and `request.event.headers` under v1 names in
its `after` hook. a proposal that demands those move has grown into a different task (F31) and
must **stop and re-scope** rather than press on.

⇒ likewise the issue deliberately types `payload` as `UnifiedApiGatewayEvent`, which keeps the
v1/v2 reconciliation. a reviewer who prefers the untouched union must raise it as a **fork**, and
that fork re-couples this to F31.

## .the four questions are YOURS to answer

the issue addresses them to the driver, so answer them in the route rather than escalate:

1. does the twin (`forAskEndpoint`) follow, or stay `{ event }`? — say it out loud either way,
   since the asymmetry is what motivated the task
2. `headers` type — reuse `UnifiedApiGatewayEvent['headers']`, never restate it
3. 🔴 **measure the real consumers before you settle the shape.** if handlers genuinely reach for
   `queryStringParameters` or `requestContext.requestId` too, four peers and no envelope may beat
   the lead proposal. the issue names this as a measurement, not a guess
4. deprecation path — a release that holds both, or a clean break. the installed base is small

⇒ each is a **fulcrum**: best-guess it, itemize it, and drive on. do not halt the road on one.

## .why it is worth a tree

- **gain.time** — the misnomer is real confusion at the busiest boundary in the package. a
  handler that wants one header takes two hops through a field whose name asserts a property the
  value does not have, and the source carries a `⚠️ .misnomer` note that says so outright
- **gain.rank** — `rule.require.ubiqlang`. a name that lies is the defect; the ergonomics win
  rides along
- the blast radius is **measured and tiny** — two prod lines and one test — which is exactly what
  makes the honest rename cheap now and expensive later, once consumers multiply

## .the bound

- **do NOT** take the spread option (C). the issue measures its collision: `body: unknown`
  (pre-validation) would sit beside `event: TInput` (post-validation) — two names for one slot at
  two pipeline stages, the very conflation the position vocabulary exists to prevent. rule it out
  **with the reason recorded**, never merely drop it
- **do NOT** lift `_` to a top-level field — the underscore marks the version-locked escape hatch,
  and to un-mark it widens the contract in silence
- **do NOT** fold in the peer task. this was kept out of
  `beav/feat-input-only-validation-and-hydration` on purpose: different behavioral impact, and no
  acceptance line behind it there
- the cors behavior owes a **clamp** that goes red if `request.event` stops to be http-shaped
  (`rule.require.clamp-edge-cases`), proven red-then-green
- the version bump must reflect a **break**
