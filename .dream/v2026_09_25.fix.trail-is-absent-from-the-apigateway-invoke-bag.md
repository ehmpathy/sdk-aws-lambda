## ⛔ do not action this dream

> *"ok then why would trail ever be on input if it should always be on context?"*
> *"absoutely not"* · *"forbid that crap"*
> — the wisher

this dream proposed to close an asymmetry — `forSqs` handed `trail` in the `invoke` bag and
`forApiGateway` did not — by a slot **added** to the family that lacked one. the wisher closed it by
the **deletion** of the two sqs slots instead: `trail` is ambient runtime, so it rides
`context.log.trail` and an `input.trail` slot is FORBIDDEN in every family
(`rule.require.env-access-in-context`, `domain.terms/trail.md`).

⇒ **to action it would re-introduce the forbidden slot.** fulcrum **F20** is dissolved with it.

## 🔴 the durable lesson

the diagnosis was right and the remedy was inverted.

`rule.forbid.defended-exceptions` grades an axis-less asymmetry as the defect itself, and this page
applied that correctly: both families minted a trail through the same `genTrailMiddleware`, both
handed a `ContextLogTrail`, and no property of http made the slot wrong at api gateway. so the
asymmetry was real.

⚠️ **what the page never asked is which arm to move.** it read the N−1 majority as the target shape
and proposed to widen the odd one out. the prior question is *"does the slot belong on ANY of
them?"* — and once `rule.require.env-access-in-context` is applied to the value rather than to the
gap, the answer is no, and the majority is what moves.

⇒ **an asymmetry names a defect; it does not name the direction of the fix.**
