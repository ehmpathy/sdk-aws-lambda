kind: fix · owner: ehmpathy/sdk-aws-lambda · caught 2026-10-02

## .the cue that fired

`enroll-impl-arch-defects` nitpick.5 both read `GenLambdaEndpointInput<TInput, TOutput, TInputBefore>`

## .the gap, precisely

`genLambdaEndpoint.forAsk.ts`'s doc-comment names `TInput` as the `inputAfter` face and cites
`rule.prefer.names-by-position-over-claim`, yet only the before face carries a position
(`TInputBefore`). `TOutput` names the output-before face with no position either.

`TInput` appears 42 times in 6 files under `src/` — `InvokeInputSuperset.ts`, `forAsk.ts`,
`getValidatedInput.ts`, `genIntrospectionMiddleware.ts`, `genZodEventValidationMiddleware.ts`, and
one test — so it is family vocabulary, not a `forAsk` local.

## .the shape of the fix

1. rename `TInput` → `TInputAfter` and `TOutput` → `TOutputBefore` in every family and shared
   middleware at once (`rule.require.consistent-variant-contracts`); `sedreplace` with `\b` bounds
2. keep `TInputBefore` as is; it already reads by position
3. `rhx git.repo.test --what types` is the whole proof — no behavior moves

## .why it is not done in this round

SAFE but not CLEAN for `fix-frozen-deep-branded-primitives`: that branch touches `forAsk.ts` for one
import, and a `forAsk`-only rename would split the family's vocabulary. fulcrum F7 records the call.
