# 🌙 dream: the review tally sub-brain is unreachable, four rounds in a row

- **kind** = `reseed` — the fix lives in `bhrain`, never in this repo
- **owner** = `bhrain` (the route guard + `rhx review`)
- **caught** = 2026-09-10

## .the cue that fired

my own i012 `.taken` named the threshold, and the fourth recurrence crossed it: a fourth occurrence
*"stops"* reading as transient and earns *"a seed to `bhrain`"*.

⇒ the cue was a **prediction i made and then met**. a diagnosis repeated four times with an
identical stderr is no longer a diagnosis; it is a report about a permanent condition, and
`rule.always.diagnose-reviewer-malfunctions` asks for one honest pass, not four.

## .the gap, precisely

every `--as arrived` on stone `5.1.execution.from_vision` since i010 has returned:

```
💥 malfunction: review tally fallback failed (the sub-brain that tallies a prose review
   could not be reached). see .agent/repo=bhrain/role=reviewer/briefs/contract.reviewer-output.md
```

| round | lane | the review body | the guard's verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| i010 | `enroll-impl-arch-defects` | full, substantive, 2 actionable items | 💥 ungradeable |
| i011 | `enroll-impl-arch-defects` | full, 2 items — both repaired | 💥 ungradeable |
| i012 | `enroll-impl-behavior-intent` | full, 4 items | 💥 ungradeable |
| i013 | `enroll-impl-behavior-intent` | full, 1 item that **re-sorted the fulcrum board** | 💥 ungradeable |

**the fault is checkable and reproduces every time.** the reviewer runs, emits a complete prose
review to stdout, and the deterministic parser then finds no numeric `N blockers` / `N nitpicks`
pair. `contract.reviewer-output` requires **both** dimensions as integers, and correctly refuses to
infer a zero — an unreadable review must not read as a clean one (`rule.forbid.failhide`).

so the guard is right to block. **what fails is the fallback**: when the deterministic parse misses,
a sub-brain is meant to tally the prose, and that sub-brain cannot be reached.

### the cost, measured

- **4 useful reviews converted into halts.** a `💥` blocks passage regardless of content, so a lane
  that did its job perfectly still stops the stone.
- **the driver pays the tally by hand.** i read each body and wrote a `.taken` per point with no
  numeric verdict to converge against — which works, and it means the guard's own convergence
  ledger (blockers closed vs. raised) has been dark for four rounds.
- **the signal is real, not noise.** i013's single recommendation moved the fulcrum board's read
  order — a rung-1 stake row had ranked tenth. that is exactly the catch a review exists for, and
  the guard could not grade it.

## 🔴 .the cause, MEASURED at i018 — the account is suspended

> this dream asked, at half 1 below: *"whether it is a model id, a timeout, a credential, or an
> endpoint, stderr names only 'could not be reached'."*
>
> ⇒ **it is a credential — an account one.** found by accident at i018, and now on record.

a scoped `rhx review`, run to repair a peer reviewer's context overflow, crashed with a raw stack
trace rather than a polite summary:

```
APIError: 412 Account vladatahbode is suspended, possibly due to reaching the monthly
spending limit or failure to pay past invoices.
Please go to https://fireworks.ai/account/billing for more information.
  code: 'PRECONDITION_FAILED'
```

⚠️ that block is a **verbatim third-party error**, so its text is a fact rather than a choice.

**`fireworks/deepseek/v4-flash` is the review skill's DEFAULT brain**, and the tally sub-brain is
the piece most likely to run on the cheap default. that fits every observation in the table above:
the review body arrives in full (the prose lane runs on its own brain) and only the small second
call fails.

⚠️ **stated at the strength it was measured at**: the `412` is observed directly; the link to the
tally sub-brain is inferred from the shape of the failure, and is unconfirmed. one read of which
brain `stepReview`'s tally path selects would settle it, and that file is in `bhrain`.

🟡 **the polite summary was worse than the crash.** the crash named the account, the URL, and the
remedy; the summary named a symptom — and i re-arrived against that symptom **six** times (i010,
i011, i012, i013, i016, i017) before an unrelated command surfaced the cause.

⇒ **a third half is therefore owed: a `412` is a CONSTRAINT, never a malfunction.** a suspended
account is caller-must-fix — no retry helps and a human holds the lever
(`rule.require.exit-code-semantics`: exit 2, not 1). to grade it a malfunction sends a driver to
diagnose a system that works as designed.

⇒ **and the human's half is now nameable**: settle the account at the url the error prints, **or
move the default to a brain that answers**. `anthropic/claude/code` works today and is what every
scoped run at i018 used. until then, 8 of the ~25 brains in the catalog are down, the default among
them.

## .the shape of the fix

two halves, and the second is the one that matters:

1. **diagnose the reach failure** — ✅ **diagnosed above at i018.** the repair is to propagate that
   cause into the message the driver actually sees, so it never again takes six rounds to find:

   ```
   💥 malfunction: review tally could not run
      ├─ cause: 412 PRECONDITION_FAILED — account suspended (fireworks)
      ├─ brain: fireworks/deepseek/v4-flash
      └─ fix: settle the account, or re-run with --brain anthropic/claude/code
   ```

   ⇒ `rule.require.errors-name-the-fix`, at the exact moment it matters most.
2. 🔴 **make the reviewer emit its own counts, so the fallback is rarely reached at all.**
   `contract.reviewer-output` already declares the canonical form, and `rhx review` conforms via
   `genReviewOutputStdout`. these `enroll-impl-*` lanes are prose reviewers that do **not**, so
   every one of them lands on the fallback by design. ⇒ **a fallback on the hot path is not a
   fallback**, and the durable repair is to have the prose lanes state their two integers rather
   than to make the tally more reliable.

⚠️ **do not "fix" this by a default of `0/0` on an unreachable tally.** that is the exact shape
`contract.reviewer-output` bars — a silent clean approval where in truth no verdict was seen.

## .why it is not done in this round

**SAFE?** ✅ — a diagnostic message and a reviewer's output format touch no product code.

**CLEAN?** 🔴 **no, and the reason is ownership rather than ripple.** both halves live in `bhrain`:
the guard's fallback path and the `enroll-impl-*` reviewer definitions. this worktree is
`ehmpathy/sdk-aws-lambda`, and a tree adopts only what is scoped to itself
(`rule.always.catch-dreams-for-followups`). there is no edit i can make here that closes it.

🟡 **and this is a `reseed`, so to catch it is not to dispatch it.** `.dream/` is a local queue — a
human or a later route must carry it to `bhrain`.
