# 🌙 dream: `genLambdaEndpoint.forSns` and `genLambdaEndpoint.forKinesis.{perRecord,perBatch}`

kind: feature · owner: sdk-aws-lambda · caught 2026-11-14

## .the cue that fired

the event-source-schemas vision (`.behavior/v2026_11_14.feat-event-source-schemas/1.vision.yield.md`
§ what is awkward) routes sns and kinesis through `forAsk`. four costs fall out of that route.

## .the gap, precisely

- `forAsk` reads a `{ event, trail }` wrapper. `invariant.ancient-vs-contemp-callers` forbids that read at an
  aws-delivered family
- `payload` means the whole envelope, so the message sits at `payload.Records[0].Sns.Message`
- kinesis refuses all or none per batch: one bad record skips every good one (F5)
- a refusal is traced only by `forAsk`'s generic caller-fault warn (F9), never a per-record line

## .the shape of the fix

copy `forSqs`. each family lifts the message to `payload` and declares a `headers` carrier
(sns `MessageAttributes`). a caller fault is acknowledged one record at a time and logged at warn.
kinesis answers `batchItemFailures` under `perBatch`. each family reuses the record-level decode that
`asLambdaEventSchema.from{Sns,Kinesis}` ships.

## .why it is not done in this round

the wish asks for schema generators only. a family is a new contract and needs its own wish.
