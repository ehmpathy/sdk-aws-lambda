### why it is a NITPICK and not a blocker

`rule.require.timeless-comments` grades a blocker only where a comment *"will actively mislead a
future reader (its reason no longer holds once the conversation is gone)"*.

not one of these misleads. each is a **provenance mark** on a claim that stands without it, and

🟡 **so the repair is a subtraction of four words per site, never a rewrite of the note.**

## .why it is not done in this round

the SAFE/CLEAN test (`rule.always.fix-forward-under-scouts-honor`):

| question | answer |
|---|---|
| **SAFE?** | ✅ comments only. no behavior, no contract, no caller |
| **CLEAN?** | 🔴 **no** — 48 judgment-calls across 20 files, at the stone's gate |

it fails CLEAN on **volume of judgment**, not on ripple. every file is one this drive already
opened, so there is no contract to disturb — but a 48-site sweep where each site needs a read of
"which half is durable" is real work, and it would land as a 20-file diff moments before the
execution stone passes, where a reviewer cannot separate it from the substance.

⇒ per the rule's own table: *"the fix is real work — a rename across files … catch it, with the
shape of the fix."*

🟡 **no fulcrum is owed.** the deferral is for SIZE, not for DIRT — there is no ripple judgment to
surface and no design call a council could overrule. the rule requires a fulcrum only where the
deferral rests on a dirt estimate.
