# 🌙 dream: one `DESERIALIZE_DEFAULT`, not three

- kind: mechanic
- owner: sdk-aws-lambda
- caught 2026-11-15

## .the cue that fired

the has-consistent-mechanisms self-review of `feat-event-source-symmetry` searched
`const DESERIALIZE_DEFAULT` across `src` and found three declarations of the same value.

## .the gap, precisely

`{ payload: true }` is declared three times, each exported:

- `src/domain.operations/asLambdaEventSchema/asLambdaEventPayloadSchema.ts:15` (typed `Readonly<…>`)
- `src/domain.operations/genLambdaEndpoint/record/asRecordBodyParsed.ts:12` (sqs, sns, kinesis)
- `src/domain.operations/genLambdaEndpoint/genLambdaEndpoint.forApiGateway/asApiGatewayRequestEventUnified.ts:117`

all three existed at `v0.8.0` (`HEAD` of the branch base); the symmetry diff moved the sqs one into
`record/` and added none. the three can drift: a change of the default at one family would leave
the others on the old value, and the `deserialize` option reads as one family-wide contract
(`rule.require.consistent-variant-contracts` lists it as one config member).

## .the shape of the fix

one declaration in `src/domain.objects/` (e.g. `LambdaEventDeserialize.ts`, beside its type
`{ payload: boolean }`), `Readonly`, imported by the three sites. no behavior change; types and the
extant suites are the clamp.

## .why it is not done in this round

it reaches `forApiGateway` and the schema face, which the symmetry diff otherwise leaves alone — a
change across families rides better in its own small pr than inside a feature diff
(`rule.always.fix-forward-under-scouts-honor`: fails CLEAN).
