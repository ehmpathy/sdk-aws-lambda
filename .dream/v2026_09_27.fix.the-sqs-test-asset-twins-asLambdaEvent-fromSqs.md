# 🌙 dream: the sqs test asset twins `asLambdaEvent.fromSqs`

fix · owner: sdk-aws-lambda · caught 2026-09-27

## .the cue that fired

the rebase onto v0.6.0 tripped `AwsIdentitySynthetic.integration.test.ts`: `src/__test_assets__/asSqsEvent.ts`
declared its own region/account literals. repaired by an import — but the literals were the symptom.

## .the gap, precisely

`src/__test_assets__/asSqsEvent.ts` builds `SQSRecord` / `SQSEvent` fixtures for the `forSqs` suites.
main shipped `asLambdaEvent.fromSqs` — a public, wire-faithful factory for the same envelope. two
builders of one aws shape can drift (`rule.forbid.parallel-codepaths`); `grep -rln "asSqsEvent'" src blackbox`
lists the consumers (5 at catch time).

## .the shape of the fix

the asset's extra value is `messageAttributes` (typed, per record) and a per-record `messageId`. either:
- widen `asLambdaEvent.fromSqs` to accept per-record `{ body, messageAttributes, messageId }`, then delete
  the asset and move its callers; or
- reduce the asset to a thin adapter over `fromSqs` that only maps its two varied facts.

prefer the widen — the public factory then serves `forSqs` authors, who need attributes too.

## .why it is not done in this round

not CLEAN: it widens a public factory's contract and touches 5 test files, in a rebase whose job is to
land the invoke-shape feat. the SAFE import repair closed the failing clamp.
